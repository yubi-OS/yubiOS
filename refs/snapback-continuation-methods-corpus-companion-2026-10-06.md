## What the corpus covers

- `01-snap-through-snap-back-limit-points.md`: limit points on the equilibrium path where tangent stiffness vanishes (dP/du = 0). Load control cannot pass a limit point; the von Mises truss shows the limit-point location is purely geometric (l_cr = (B^2 L_0)^(1/3), no E or A in it). Snap-back is the complementary mechanism: the branch reverses in displacement while load still changes, and a shallow-roof benchmark shows the full sequence (load peaks near 590 N, unloads through zero at about -17 mm, snaps back with load reversing to about -400 N).
- `02-riks-arc-length-continuation.md`: the arc-length constraint Delta u^T Delta u + Phi Delta lambda^2 (F^T F) = Delta s^2 treats load and displacement jointly so the solve passes limit points. Wempner 1971 and Riks 1972 originated it independently; Crisfield's 1981 reformulation (quadratic in the load correction with a root-selection rule) is what production codes implement. ABAQUS Riks and ANSYS ARCLEN carry it in production.
- `03-stability-criteria-bifurcation.md`: the Trefftz energy criterion (sign of the second variation), the topological difference between bifurcation (two branches intersect, det K_T = 0 both cases) and limit-point instability (a turning point of one path), Koiter's initial post-buckling slope as the imperfection-sensitivity verdict, and the linearized eigenvalue problem as the practical test.
- `04-creep-buckling-viscoelastic-stability.md`: failure at a finite time t* below the instantaneous elastic critical load. The elastic-viscoelastic correspondence principle does not transfer to stability: Urbach and Efrati's two-result theory shows stability assessed at a locally stable arrival instant is permanent, and stability acquired through relaxation is always transient. Viscoelastic stability depends on full relaxation history, not any single effective modulus.
- `05-tracking-unstable-branches-computational.md`: branch switching via buckling-eigenmode imperfections (with the documented pitfall that the perturbation can converge back to the trivial branch), predictor-corrector Newton correctors, limit-point detection, and Abaqus automatic stabilization, where excess damping masks the instability the analysis exists to find, so ALLSD/ALLIE energy checks are the guardrail.
- `06-hysteresis-recovery-measured-loops.md`: loop area is dissipated energy, reducing to W_dis = pi sigma0 eps0 sin(delta) in the linear-viscoelastic limit. Recovery fraction separates elastic return from permanent set. The Mullins effect softens against the prior maximum and partly recovers thermally. Practice: controlled rates, hold-based permanent-set separation, energy-accounting cross-checks.

## Key findings distilled

1. What snap-back means in the sign-gate program. Mechanically it is the continuation branch reversing: load rises, displacement falls back. Round 3 of the jev-corpus RSI chain was exactly this: 10 cycles of axis-fill edits (load rose, matrix cells flipped) while measured structure fell (realized delta went +0.64 against negative-direction predictions; level_dbc moved -11.11 to -10.46; PR #278 closed UNMERGED). The corpus grounds both halves of that analogy: doc 01's fold-back definition, and doc 04's history-dependence, which is why the gate reads the full series rather than a single audit.
2. The continuation-method framing. Doc 02's lesson is that load control stalls at a limit point and displacement control loses a snap-back; the fix is treating load and displacement as joint unknowns. The program analog reads predicted_delta and realized_delta jointly: the (predicted, realized) pair as a bearing, aligned + meaningful = keep, inverted = the edit moved the corpus against the geometry (revert).
3. The halt_round gate decision. Doc 05's central pitfall, damping masking true instability, maps to the gate's design: the snapback detector returns verdicts only, never auto-actions, and the prose sign-gate rule remains the fallback. A `{"verdict":"snapback","gate_input":{"action":"halt_round"}}` response is a hard stop: halt the round, revert the edit, record the negative result, re-lens. The detector mechanizes the runbook prose (missing link L2 in `knowledge/linear-viscoelasticity/00-ideate-and-missing-links.md`) instead of leaving the round-3 lesson as discipline.
4. The hysteresis half. Doc 06 grounds the rollup: loop area as dissipated effort per edit cycle, recovery fraction near 1 as elastic (the edit changed state, not structure) and near 0 as plastic (real structural change). Round-3 calibration: 102.86 dBc-units of prediction-vs-realized dissipation over 10 cycles.
