## Problem Statement
How might we combine all 18 TimesFM, Clairic, Clef, and Jev reference architectures with our empirical lessons from 2,645 Polymarket/WAE forecasts into two specialized workers (`refinery.systems-a.workers.dev` and `forecaster.systems-a.workers.dev`) and a unified WAE/Forecasts hub page on `steadyorbitsystems.com` without duplicating state or blocking UI interactivity on 18.6-second Workers AI decision calls?

## Recommended Direction
We converge on **Variation 6 (The Closed-Loop Triad)**, which cleanly separates **Upstream Data & Covariate Refinement (`refinery.systems-a.workers.dev`)**, **Downstream Multi-Path Forecasting & Arbitrage Execution (`forecaster.systems-a.workers.dev`)**, and **Canonical API, WAE Trajectory Storage & Public Showcase (`steady-orbit` at `steadyorbitsystems.com/forecasts/`)**.

In this architecture, **`refinery.systems-a.workers.dev`** acts as the active intake and enrichment studio: it ingests raw WAE trade ticks, CSVs, and Polymarket/market questions; extracts tight search spans via **Clairic** (`jev-search`); queries our internal **SearXNG** webhook (`searxng-proxy`); suppresses low-signal floods and deduplicates snippets via **Clef** + **`jevgrep` admission gates**; extracts 9-type structured temporal covariates via **Clairic** (`OpenComputerUse`); resamples irregular ticks and scrubs $3\hat{\sigma}$ quantile anomalies (`Zer0Fit` + `timesfm3-studio`); and measures **Covariate Lift ($\Delta\text{MASE}$)** before committing refined series and covariates to `steady-orbit` (`/api/jev/timeseries/import` and D1 `refined_series`).

**`forecaster.systems-a.workers.dev`** upgrades the proven Sauna Forecaster app into a standalone Cloudflare Worker terminal backed by `steady-orbit`'s Cascade, ETS, Clairic, and WAE APIs. It keeps the 60fps Dual-Switch Distribution Map (`Group By Category / Time Window` $\times$ `Consensus / Cascade / ETS / Clairic + 4-Path Overlay`) and adds four capabilities learned from our audit and the 18 repos: (1) **Von Scale-Invariant Margin Confidence** ($\text{conf} = (n p_{\max}-1)/(n-1)$) + **Split-Conformal Quantile Bands**, (2) **Kalman Filter Dynamic Beta & Strike-Ladder Arbitrage Scanner** (`statistical-arb-timesfm`), (3) **Interactive What-If Covariate Shock Sandbox** (`timesfm3-studio`), and (4) **One-Click "Send to Refinery"** for sparse ($n<8$) or high-divergence markets. Finally, **`steadyorbitsystems.com/forecasts/`** (KV key `forecasts.html` + `/api/jev/refinery/*` on `steady-orbit`) serves as the public/operator WAE trajectory & live accuracy showcase in the Steady Orbit v19 brand frame.

## Not Doing (and Why)
- **Not running PyTorch/CUDA weights inside Cloudflare Workers** — Workers have a 128 MB memory limit and CPU time caps; heavy neural checkpoints run via Workers AI bindings (`@cf/cloudflare/clairic`, `@cf/cloudflare/clef`) or the deterministic parity kernel (`jev-cascade-forecast.js`), with optional local GPU/CPU offload via `Zer0Fit`/`ollaya` on `ubuntu` only when explicitly requested.
- **Not vendoring `WTFPL` (`docker-predictalot`) or unlicensed (`statistical-arb-timesfm`, `RayCodes_*`, `luandro/clef`, `rmax-ai/jev-vs-clef`) code verbatim** — all algorithms from those repos are implemented clean-room from mathematical specifications to preserve `yubi-OS` license hygiene.
- **Not exposing internal codenames (`jev`, `n8n`, `google-research`) in user-facing UI labels** — per the 2026-10-24 branding rule in `RULES.md`, all user-facing titles and labels useSteady Orbit functional terminology (`Ops & Decision Engine`, `DAG Search Proxy`, `3-Stage Cascade`, `System-One Classifier`).
