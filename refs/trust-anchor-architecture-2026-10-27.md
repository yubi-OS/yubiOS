## 12-Axis Negative Skill Space (NSS) Enrichment Specification

- **Audience:** Systems architects, security engineers, operators, and maintainer personas auditing or implementing trust-anchor architecture.
- **Inputs:** Hardware root-of-trust parameters, digest-pinned container configs, environment variables, and signed policy manifests (`jev-policy.json`, OPA/Rego).
- **Outputs:** Deterministic allow/deny gate decisions, signed UKI/dm-verity measurements, structured JSONL audit logs, and verified exit codes.
- **Mode:** One-shot preflight verification and continuous runtime daemon enforcement with fail-closed errexit semantics.
- **Assumption Set:** Precondition invariants require un-exportable hardware key material (YubiKey PIV slot 9c / FIDO2) and a measured boot chain.
- **Adjacent Problems:** Contrasted against legacy TPM-only OEM trust chains, mutable package-managed roots, and unverified runtime containers (prior art versus structural verification).
- **Failure Modes:** Key revocation, digest mismatch, rollback attacks, TOCTOU race conditions, and partial-write hazards fail closed with explicit error codes.
- **Lifecycle:** Versioned under SemVer 2.0.0 with ADR-tracked deprecation, changelog provenance, and atomic `bootc` A/B rollback.
- **Composition:** Pairs with `dm-verity`, `composefs`, `mkosi`, `bcvk`, OPA/Rego build policies, and Sigstore Rekor v2 transparency logs.
- **Knowledge Sources:** Grounded in `yubi-OS/knowledge`, `yubi-OS/yubiOS` ADRs, NIST SP 800-207, and RFC/kernel reference documentation (https://docs.kernel.org/filesystems/overlayfs.html, https://github.com/yubi-OS/knowledge).
- **Calibration:** Evaluated against a `0.55` Clef relevance threshold, 12-axis NSS coverage (`>= 0.85`), and curveball null-model z-score calibration.
- **Recursion:** Closed-loop self-archaeology and RSI cycle feedback reflect operational lessons back into `jev_harness_library`.

## 10-Primitive yubiOS Structural Mapping

- **P0 Attestation:** Remote and local quote verification over measured boot PCRs and signed evidence bundles.
- **P1 Trust Chain:** Rooted in owner-controlled hardware (YubiKey PIV / FIDO2), eliminating unverified third-party anchors.
- **P2 Least Privilege:** Strict capability bounding, rootless buildx/podman execution, and isolated namespaces.
- **P3 Declarative Policy:** Fail-closed OPA/Rego and Policy v27 rules evaluated prior to any state mutation.
- **P4 Continuous / Adaptive:** Continuous runtime detection and telemetry over immutable system state.
- **P5 Immutability:** Read-only `composefs` / `dm-verity` root filesystems with digest-pinned base images.
- **P6 Audit / Evidence:** Hash-chained, tamper-evident event logs anchored to Rekor v2.
- **P7 Cryptographic Identity:** Hardware-resident FIDO2 and PIV keys for signing, SSH, LUKS2, and PAM.
- **P8 Segmentation:** Microsegmented container, VM (`bcvk`), and hardware domain isolation.
- **P9 Self-Describing:** Typed schemas, embedded provenance attestations, and machine-verifiable manifests.
