# linear-viscoelasticity corpus companion (2026-10-06)

The corpus at `knowledge/linear-viscoelasticity/` (7 docs, minted 2026-10-02 in yubi-OS/knowledge) is the knowledge base behind reading the yubiOS corpus-laying harness as a viscoelastic material: rigidity (frozen frame), creep and relaxation (metric drift under sustained directive streams), dissipation (predict-vs-realized hysteresis), and snap-back (round-3 style sign reversals). Anchor doc: D. Roylance, Engineering Viscoelasticity, MIT 2001, 37 pp, verified by direct PDF read. This doc is the refs-side mirror; it distills what the corpus claims, what it found, and where the shipped instruments stand.

## What the corpus covers

- `01-molecular-mechanisms-regimes.md`: energetic vs entropic elasticity, glassy modulus Eg near 3 GPa vs rubbery modulus Er, Tg, free volume, crosslink density (Er = 3NRT), the thermoelastic diagnostic. One correction is recorded in-doc: the corpus brief stated sigma = NRT(lambda − 1/lambda); the verified PDF reads lambda − 1/lambda squared.
- `02-creep-relaxation-dynamic-tests.md`: the 3 canonical tests. Creep compliance Ccrp(t), relaxation modulus Erel(t), their exact reciprocal endpoints (Eg = 1/Cg, Er = 1/Cr) but non-reciprocal full functions, and the dynamic split into storage E', loss E'', and tan delta = E''/E', with Wdis/Wst = 2 pi tan delta.
- `03-spring-dashpot-models.md`: Maxwell, Kelvin-Voigt, Standard Linear Solid, Wiechert/Prony. Key general result: retardation time exceeds relaxation time by the factor (ke + k1)/ke, the glassy-to-rubbery modulus ratio. Erel(t) = ke + sum kj exp(−t/tau_j) is the Prony series the instrument later fits.
- `04-boltzmann-superposition-correspondence.md`: the hereditary integral, 4 equivalent Duhamel forms, Erel * Ccrp = t (convolution inverses), the viscoelastic correspondence principle (Lee 1962), and what viscoelasticity does not change (statically determinate stress fields).
- `05-time-temperature-superposition.md`: thermorheologically simple materials, the shift factor aT, Arrhenius and WLF forms (C1 = 17.4, C2 = 51.6 at Tr = Tg, from Williams, Landel, Ferry JACS 77:3701-3707, 1955), master curves, and effective time t' = integral of dxi/aT(xi).
- `06-lean-mathlib-formalization-path.md`: what mathlib4 has (convolution in `Mathlib/Analysis/Convolution.lean`, ODE existence/uniqueness, matrix exponential, Mellin transform) and what it lacks (Laplace transform, Volterra/Titchmarsh convolution inverse, named Duhamel, Prony). Verified against the mathlib4 repo on 2026-10-03. 4 theorem candidates T1-T4, with honest sizing: the general Volterra-inverse development is multi-month; the exponential-family special case is weeks.
- `07-measurement-practice-reference-canon.md`: DMA practice (E', E'', tan delta), master-curve construction, collocation fitting of Prony series, and an 11-entry verified bibliography (Ferry, Christensen, Tschoegl, Findley, WLF 1955, Roylance).
- `00-ideate-and-missing-links.md`: the ideate-solo one-pager and missing-links analysis, both jev-qualified. Lives inside the corpus, unlike the 01-07 knowledge docs.
- `08-creep-recovery-replay.md`: the executed MVP instrument test (detailed below).
- `research-db/`: preflight.json (searXNG probe: 118 results via `?endpoint=search&qs=`, jev /api/decide smoke), digs/01-07.json (per-doc dig results with jev weights), archive.json (collection summary: 24, 12, 15, 0, 12, 0, 12 weighted results per doc), qc-log.json (every /api/decide call), db.ts. There is no outline.json in this corpus; archive.json and db.ts are the index layer.

## Key findings distilled

The mapping (from `00-ideate-and-missing-links.md`, argued as same-shaped objects, not metaphor): frozen frame (rule_hash, baseline_id, frozen task check) = glassy modulus Eg, the instant reversible regime. Coverage-matrix flips = plastic state: cells stay flipped while measured level regresses, the round-3 trap. Predict-vs-realized ledger rows = load-strain data: predicted_delta is load, realized_delta is strain, supersedes chains are the unloading path. Policy version = the WLF shift factor: policy v1 to v5 changed response rates (allowed methods, validators) without changing the moduli (gate shape, terminal states). The structural gift: heat-kernel modes already decay as E_l(t) = E_l(0) exp(−2l(l+1)t) (papers/README.md F9), the same exp(−t/tau) family as a Prony arm, so a relaxation spectrum is a fit in coordinates the stack already computes.

The missing links L1-L6: L1, no time-dependent instrument at all (creep, relaxation, dynamic moduli have no /jev/ counterpart; audit history exists in runs but nothing fits it). L2, no snap-back detector; the round-3 sign lesson is runbook prose, not a fail-closed gate input. L3, no `/api/jev/corpus/visco` Prony surface (K=2 minimum, 30 audit points across rounds 1-3 already falsifiable). L4, no dissipation metric on the ledger: hysteresis area per supersedes chain is computable, nobody integrates it. L5, policy version is an unexploited shift factor; if shape changes across a policy bump the thermorheologically-simple assumption itself is falsifiable. L6, tier-2 swap gains a time layer: an instantaneous DifficultyZone score cannot distinguish elastic reverting from plastically hardening environments; score the lag and the recovery fraction instead.

The MVP creep-recovery replay (`08-creep-recovery-replay.md`, corpus pinned at round-3 base SHA 379a9f2a, 244 refs/ docs, 8 grader lanes, 200-null audits) returned findings F1-F5:

- F1: scorer variance dominates the gate, 5.77 dBc. The replayed baseline sits at −16.88 where the recorded pass read −11.11 at the same SHA; the round was gated on a +0.64 effect inside that band. Pin the scorer, re-score only edited rows, report variance per round.
- F2: R is trivial under deterministic scoring. The text-revert unload returns the matrix exactly, recovery fraction R = 1.0 for every edit class. The elastic/plastic split does not live in the revert leg.
- F3: persistence is the discriminating measurement, 9/9. A blind independent grader credits all 9 applied flips from the loaded text, and measured level moves further from baseline, +3.22 dBc. The axis-fills were substantive; the regression was corpus-level response direction.
- F4: the load sign is a frame property. The replay load leg (+2.57) reproduces round 3's sign (+0.64): in this frame filling cells moves dBc toward the null mechanically.
- F5: prediction-vs-realized hysteresis is large and measurable. Lens candidates expected +9.79 to +11.50 per cycle against realized deltas summing to +0.65; sum |predicted − realized| = 102.9 dBc-units across 10 cycles, mean 10.29 per cycle. That is the dissipation metric the outcomes ledger was designed to catch.

Jev qualification of the replay (task ta2687dc): persist_beats_R 0.92, gate_noise_risk 0.89.

## Relationship to the shipped instruments

`08-creep-recovery-replay.md` is the corpus's own MVP executed, not a proposal: its findings are the calibration case. The code shipped in `tools/visco-instruments/` (yubiOS repo: CONTRACTS.md, verify_visco.py, generate_fixtures.py, fixtures/visco-fixtures.json) is the Python source of record for the two numbers the replay validated, `persistence_stats` and `hysteresis_rollup`, with the JS port `jev-visco-math.js` coding against frozen fixtures. CONTRACTS.md encodes the replay lessons directly as contracts: dBc improvement is more negative, deltas consumed exactly as recorded with only signs compared, persistence direction fixed at base bit 0 to 1, and the replay's load leg delta (+2.566) pinned as a fixture-level sign convention. Per doc 08's implication 1, the persistence leg leads and R follows; R becomes non-trivial only with a rate-dependent or stochastic scorer. The L3 `/api/jev/corpus/visco` Prony surface remains the ship-order item behind these two, and the policy-as-temperature study (L5) is analysis, no code.
