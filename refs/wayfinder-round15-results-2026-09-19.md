## Generator exhaustion (the round's main finding)

The ladder generator's candidate set shrank as the chain progressed: 3 rungs at baseline 536 (all
executed by cycle 3), the same 3 at maps 538/539 (all attempted), and **0 rungs** on a re-baseline
(map 540, frame `b66db1f0ded812f9`, refit on the current 233-doc corpus, disclosed per the round-8
re-baseline precedent). On the refit frame the isolated count dropped 49 → 34: the corpus is
materially better connected than the round-15 baseline frame suggested, and the generator offers
no further favorable one-row addition or one-bit change. This is the honest end state of
rung-per-cycle on this corpus: **rounds 13–15 have consumed the rung-realizable work.** The 15
remaining frozen-check failures are real but live outside the generator's candidate set — they
were never named by any ladder, and closing them is sweep work (the round-14 pattern), not
wayfinding.

## Driver defect found and fixed (affects round 14's ledger, not its commits)

Round 15's first driver build rebuilt the after-map corpus from the baseline name list only, so
**ADD-cycle after-maps were no-op maps** (round 15 cycle 2's first after-map, map 537, excluded
the new census document entirely; the crash it then hit surfaced the bug). Fixed before any
further cycles; the cycle-2 reading above (map 538) is the corrected measurement. **The same
defect existed in round 14**: its three ADD cycles (1–3) recorded after-maps 438/439/440 and
observed deltas of 0 from maps that did not contain the added documents — those three geometry
readings are void and should be read as no-measurement; the round-14 commits themselves are
unaffected (the documents are on main and pass the frozen check).
