## Ledger operating modes

The ledger's operating mode is append-only, one-shot per row, never a rewrite. A cycle registers a prediction once (pre-registration), the verdict row lands after the independent check, and the pending row is superseded rather than mutated; the round-11 snapshot states that pending rows stay pending until their verdict rows supersede them. Re-registering the same prediction is not an idempotent overwrite: round 8's re-baseline precedent shows the honest handling of a do-over is disclosure, not a silent second row mixed in (map 193's re-baseline at frame `10f0496abde9aab9` is "disclosed in the round's records", with stale-baseline rows marked by their baselines, not silently mixed). Unknowns are handled by keeping them pending, not by converting them to counts of another class; the round-11 mid-round ledger read that returned zero rows was recorded in the cycle-29 snapshot as a read-time artifact to re-verify at round close, not treated as evidence about the ledger. The counts-only contract means error/unknown handling never upgrades to rates: round 10's frozen-check failures (8/21 at baseline) and round 11's 0/21 appear as task-check facts, not ledger verdict statistics. Checks amended mid-round must be versioned and recorded in the ledger (lesson 26, from round 7's v1.1 amendment). The read side has two modes: per-baseline (one chain link) and per-frame (all rows on the frozen frame), both declared, both returning real row sets or zero rows, with zero rows from the baseline-scoped read on a chained round being an expected artifact.
