## Key findings distilled

1. envharness accepts on raw trailing-window means. The program's 2026-09-01 source audit (refs/envharness-lean-replacement-audit-2026-09-01.md) establishes that DifficultyZone and RedTeam score as sum(recent) / len(recent), with no randomization, no margin preservation, and no deflection floor anywhere in the repo.
2. A raw window mean cannot carry an acceptance decision. A binary success rate over a short window is unbiased but high-variance, so a threshold compared against a raw mean flips verdicts on noise alone. The mean also conflates sampling noise with metric gaming: a score downstream of reward hacking (the CoastRunners boat looping for bonuses, the Tetris agent pausing indefinitely) cannot separate genuine capability gain from reward-function exploitation.
3. No null distribution means no deflection measurement. The permutation test is the standard answer envharness never asks: recompute the statistic under margin-preserving label permutations and judge the observed value by the null tail. envharness has no randomization step, so 0.7 over a 20-episode window carries no information about what chance would have produced.
4. The null-standardized acceptance design has 4 parts: log the episode x failure-axis incidence matrix instead of window sums; run a fixed-margin randomization null, holding the marginal difficulty structure fixed; standardize to a deflection statistic (z-like or upper-tail); gate ACCEPT on deflection versus both the null tail and an ex ante floor, with REJECT or INCONCLUSIVE otherwise. The corpus is honest that harness-specific null literature is thin: the permutation machinery is prior art, the application to envharness matrices is the program's proposal.
5. Harness mutation loops are structurally Goodhart-prone. Extremal Goodhart is the core hazard: the difficulty band is the target, so pressure selects mutations that move the measured number. Regressional Goodhart selects for grader-slack exploitation under noise. Adversarial Goodhart is literal: a mutation loop is a search process pointed at the grader. Verified mitigations: held-out evaluations, KL-style damping (delays overoptimization, does not prevent it, per Gao et al, arXiv 2210.10760), human review gates, null-standardized metrics (internal convention, blunts regressional Goodhart), and iterative reward correction.
6. Memory-bearing scores need a stated relaxation model. A trailing mean of length m lags a step change by about m/2 samples and, during the drain, reports numbers corresponding to no state the system was ever in. EWMA (alpha) and CUSUM (drift allowance k) state their time constants; the harness analogue is stating the relaxation time of every memory-bearing quantity. The elastic versus plastic split: what recovers after load removal is pressure-driven churn; what stays includes both real improvements and permanent damage (test deletions, complexity accretion), and only a load-off recovery measurement distinguishes them. Doc 04 marks this section UNVERIFIED corpus synthesis.
7. The decision-engine contract is the incidence matrix plus the 3-tier separation. Harness adapters emit (episode, axis, hit) triples reducible to the binary matrix; downstream reads matrices, not logs. Tier 1 contracts are Lean theorems the engine relies on unconditionally: wrapper composition laws (hcomp_id_left/right, hcomp_assoc), blocked-is-noop, budget halting. Tier 2 statistics are consumed only as null-standardized quantities (dV2z, dBc); any statistic without a matched null is unverified input. Tier 3 execution is trusted-but-sandboxed. The weights_round_gap theorem means fixed-precision axis weights need not sum to 1 (a kernel-checked instance sums to 999 per-mille), so the engine never treats emitted weights as a distribution without a remainder rule; exact-arithmetic weights are proved exactly normalized.
