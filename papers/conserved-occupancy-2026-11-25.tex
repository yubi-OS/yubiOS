\begin{abstract}
A powered chart may separate observations without improving a classifier. We connect four yubiOS papers through an experiment in which geometry assigns each observation fractions of six mechanisms and the fractions sum to its measured amount. The construction parallels the two steps of equilibrium counting: convert a sample into a count of specified entities, then apportion that count with normalized Boltzmann weights. Two observed Cloudflare BGE-M3 passes cover 108 authored sensor notes from 18 instruments. On two held-out sets of 36, a reference-fitted spherical dilation increased greedy packing from 3 to 7 and 6 to 11 at a fixed chord. Temperature-adjusted Gibbs recognition rose from 21 to 22 and 23 to 26. The paired station tests gave $p=1.00$ and $0.50$. Native centroids recognized 24 and 25. In a four-bit representation, 181 of 630 query pairs collided, while no individual Walsh degree passed the blocked class-label test. The powered allocation of 4,856 text bytes on the second holdout assigned 928.88 bytes to drift. These outcomes distinguish resolution, resemblance and recognition in one reproducible measurement.
\end{abstract}

\section{Four instruments, one experiment}
Paper I fits periodic curves to learned coordinates. A fitted curve can describe recurring structure even when its coefficients depend on the sampling of the curve. Paper II asks whether a measured feature survives a matched null, and organizes configurations into energy shells. Paper III and the papers README connect angular spectra to chart choice and heat flow. Paper IV counts the distinguishable positions remaining at a specified resolution and selects a prefix of a binary frame. Together these operations raise a question left open by counting alone: can a query assign more evidence to its known mechanism than to other mechanisms on an unseen instrument? \citep{p1,p2,p3,p4,readme}

We authored one note for each combination of 18 instruments and six mechanisms: clipping, drift, gain, lag, offset and quantization. The mechanism is the target label. Instrument identity is the blocking variable. We embedded the text field alone. Six instruments supplied 36 references, six supplied holdout A and six supplied a fresh holdout B. The two query sets are disjoint from each other and from the reference instruments. Templates, author and domain remain shared. B tests transfer to other authored instruments within this design. Words in a note may reveal a mechanism directly. This study does not measure recognition of independently collected physical faults.

\section{The amount that multiplies a Boltzmann fraction}
For a pure substance of mass $m$ and molar mass $M$, $n=m/M$ is its amount in moles. One mole contains exactly $6.02214076\times10^{23}$ specified entities. The corresponding conversion factor is $N_A=6.02214076\times10^{23}\ {\rm mol}^{-1}$. For molecules with single-molecule levels $\epsilon_i$, degeneracies $g_i$, and temperature $T>0$, the canonical model gives \citep{si,tong}
\begin{align}
 N&=nN_A,& Z&=\sum_i g_i e^{-\epsilon_i/(k_BT)},&
 p_i&=g_i e^{-\epsilon_i/(k_BT)}/Z,& \E N_i&=Np_i.\label{eq:mole}
\end{align}
For a mixture apply the mass conversion separately to each species. With $E_i^{\rm mol}=N_A\epsilon_i$ and $R=N_A k_B$, $E_i^{\rm mol}/(RT)=\epsilon_i/(k_BT)$. Degeneracy can enter as an effective energy $\epsilon_i-k_BT\log g_i$. These are canonical ensemble weights. The kinetic Boltzmann transport equation addresses a different physical problem.

For a query $q$ and reference bank $R_c$ of size $n_c>0$, choose an energy $E(q,r)$ and bandwidth $\tau>0$. With $\pi_c\ge0$ and at least one positive prior define
\begin{equation}
 s_c(q)=\frac{\pi_c}{n_c}\sum_{r\in R_c}e^{-E(q,r)/\tau},
 \qquad p_c(q)=s_c(q)\big/\sum_b s_b(q).                 \label{eq:alloc}
\end{equation}
Energy on a unit sphere is $1-q^\top r=\|q-r\|^2/2$. On the cube we use Hamming distance. Equal priors were used in the study. On the sphere the exponential kernel is proportional to a directional kernel in $q^\top r$ \citep{zhang}. The probabilities are normalized resemblance scores. Their calibration as predictive probabilities would require independent validation. Replicating a whole bank $k$ times multiplies its sum and size by $k$. With the chart, bandwidth and priors frozen its mean is invariant. Raw sums instead encode a frequency prior. Genuine shell multiplicities, class priors and duplicated records therefore need different accounting.

\begin{proposition}[Conservation of a measured amount]
Give query $q$ an amount $a_q$. Then $A_c=\sum_q a_qp_c(q)$ satisfies $\sum_c A_c=\sum_q a_q$. In particular write $U_c=\sum_qp_c(q)$ for note-equivalents and $B_c=\sum_q b_qp_c(q)$ for allocated UTF-8 text bytes. For $U_c>0$, $\overline b_c=B_c/U_c$ has units bytes per assigned note-equivalent.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}Interchange the finite sums and apply $\sum_c p_c(q)=1$ separately to every query. Multiplying $\overline b_c$ by $U_c$ recovers $B_c$.\end{proof}
Equation~\eqref{eq:mole} motivates the separation of amount from allocation. $\overline b_c$ is a weighted mean of the observed lengths. It changes with the corpus, encoding or scorer. The reference bank has no physical molar mass, temperature bath or demonstrated thermal equilibrium.

The existing Boltzmann-collapse tool makes the multiplicity explicit. For an exchangeable potential $\Phi(j)$ on the whole cube,
\[
 \pi_\tau(j)=\binom dj e^{-\Phi(j)/\tau}/Z,\qquad
 F_\tau(j)=\Phi(j)-\tau\log\binom dj.
\]
For $\Phi(j)=j$, $Z=(1+r)^d$ with $r=e^{-1/\tau}$. At $d=4,\tau=0.75$ the five ideal shell probabilities are $(0.3923,0.4136,0.1635,0.0287,0.0019)$. The tool independently builds the 16-state Metropolis chain. Its stationary shell probabilities agreed with the closed form to machine precision (maximum absolute difference $<10^{-15}$ in the reported runs). For the empirical bank use $H_{qcj}=\#\{r'\in R_c:d_H(q,r')=j\}$ in place of $\binom dj$. Then $s_c(q)=(\pi_c/n_c)\sum_j H_{qcj}r^j$. This distinction keeps the combinatorics of the possible states separate from the instruments actually observed.

\section{Curvature, scale and resolution}
Let $p$ be the normalized reference mean and $x\in S^{D-1}$, $x\ne-p$. Orthogonal projection followed by a stereographic chart, dilation and inverse chart gives
\begin{equation}
 w(x)=\frac{x-(p^\top x)p}{1+p^\top x},\quad
 L_s(x)=\frac{2sw(x)+(1-s^2\|w(x)\|^2)p}{1+s^2\|w(x)\|^2},\quad
 s=\left(|R|^{-1}\sum_{r\in R}\|w(r)\|^2\right)^{-1/2}.\label{eq:lens}
\end{equation}
The two numerator terms are perpendicular, so $4u+(1-u)^2=(1+u)^2$ for $u=s^2\|w\|^2$ proves unit norm. $L_1$ is identity and $L_{1/s}$ reverses $L_s$. The planar map uses the opposite pole convention, with last coordinate $(u-1)/(u+1)$. The ambient experiment uses all 1,024 coordinates. The PCA globe uses two planar coordinates lifted to $S^2$. Its density and angular coefficients can change even when labels and distinct codes stay fixed. In the ambient experiment we multiply $\tau=0.05$ by the ratio of powered to native median reference pair energies. Uniform energy scaling would cancel exactly in $E/\tau$. A stereographic dilation generally scales different pairs by different factors.

On a sphere of radius $R_s$, right geodesic legs of lengths $a,b$ satisfy $\cos(c/R_s)=\cos(a/R_s)\cos(b/R_s)$ \citep{green,sphere}. On the unit sphere, writing $E_a=1-\cos a$ gives
\begin{equation}
 E_c=E_a+E_b-E_aE_b. \label{eq:curve}
\end{equation}
An octant triangle has $a=b=c=\pi/2$, so $a^2+b^2>c^2$. Greenwald's string experiment illustrates this departure from the plane. For a numerical control we took the first two A queries' angular distances from $p$, preserved both lengths, and orthogonalized their tangent directions at $p$. The legs were 0.8464 and 0.8032 radians, yielding $a^2+b^2-c^2=0.167717$ rad$^2$. This measures a constructed right angle. It makes no assertion about the angle between the two original semantic directions.

For prefix length $j$, let $C_j$ be occupied code count, $D_0(j)=\log_2 C_j$, and $\Delta_j=D_0(j)-D_0(j-1)$. Gains telescope. Paper IV stops at the first gain below half a bit. The nine reference prefix counts were $2,4,6,9,12,16,24,29,32$, giving $j^\star=4$. With empirical frequencies $h_x/N$ the collision effective count is $2^{D_2}=1/\sum_x(h_x/N)^2$. Greedy packing at chord $\varepsilon$ retains points in order when each is at least $\varepsilon$ from every earlier retained point. Its count lower-bounds the maximum packing number. The representation, radius and ordering must accompany a packing result.

\section{Three Fourier operations and the multiplicity test}
A sampled curve with coefficients $\beta_i$ and sampled basis $\Phi_{qi}$ has power
\begin{equation}
 \|\Phi\beta\|^2=\beta^\top G\beta,\quad G=\Phi^\top\Phi.
\end{equation}
If $G=NI$, this equals $N\sum_i\beta_i^2$. Arbitrarily sampled learned frequencies may have cross terms. On $S^2$, orthonormal surface harmonics have $\Delta Y_{\ell m}=-\ell(\ell+1)Y_{\ell m}$ and $2\ell+1$ modes at degree $\ell$. Heat time $t$ multiplies an amplitude by $e^{-t\ell(\ell+1)}$, hence power by $e^{-2t\ell(\ell+1)}$. The continuous heat kernel is
\[
 K_t(x^\top y)=\sum_{\ell\ge0}\frac{2\ell+1}{4\pi}
 e^{-t\ell(\ell+1)}P_\ell(x^\top y).
\]
The README relates this to the great-circle interpolant
$\big(\sin((1-u)\theta)x+\sin(u\theta)y\big)/\sin\theta$ for $0<\theta<\pi$ \citep{readme,shoemake}. A single deterministic interpolation is a geodesic segment. Random walks with a specified transition law can model diffusion. With $\hat c_{\ell m}=N^{-1}\sum_q Y_{\ell m}(q)$ and $P_\ell=\sum_m|\hat c_{\ell m}|^2$, we measured $P_\ell/\sum_{k=0}^3P_k$ through degree three; we did not fit a heat time to the powered globe.

On the $d$-bit cube let $\chi_S(x)=(-1)^{S\cdot x}$, $\widehat f(S)=\sum_x f(x)\chi_S(x)$, and $f(x)=2^{-d}\sum_S\widehat f(S)\chi_S(x)$ \citep{odonnell}. The Hamming kernel $k(x)=r^{|x|}$ has $r=e^{-1/\tau}$. Multiplying one-bit transforms gives
\begin{equation}
 \widehat k(S)=(1+r)^{d-j}(1-r)^j,\quad
 \widehat k(S)/\widehat k(\varnothing)=((1-r)/(1+r))^j,\quad j=|S|.\label{eq:walsh}
\end{equation}
There are exactly $\binom dj$ masks of degree $j$. For the normalized class histogram $h_c$, $k*h_c$ is the direct Hamming Gibbs mean. Its degree-$j$ contribution is
\begin{equation}
 u_{jc}(q)=2^{-d}\sum_{|S|=j}\widehat k(S)\widehat h_c(S)\chi_S(q).\label{eq:signed}
\end{equation}
Normalization across classes follows summation over degrees. Individual contributions may be negative. With a raw whole-corpus histogram $h$, the distinct-index pair count at Hamming separation $t$ instead uses squared coefficients,
\begin{align}
 K_t(j)&=\sum_i(-1)^i \binom ji\binom{d-j}{t-i},\\
 H_t&=\tfrac12\left[2^{-d}\sum_S\widehat h(S)^2K_t(|S|)-N\,{\bf1}_{t=0}\right].\label{eq:pairs}
\end{align}
Out-of-range binomial coefficients are zero. In particular $H_0=\sum_x h_x(h_x-1)/2$. The same representation supplies an unsigned measure of clustering and a signed test of mechanism alignment.

A multiplicity argument alone cannot establish a finite amount. With $2^n$ states at level $n$ and weight $w_n=m_0/[2^n(n+1)]$ per state,
\begin{equation}
 \sum_{n<L}2^nw_n=m_0\sum_{n<L}(n+1)^{-1},\qquad
 \sum_{n\ge0}2^n w_n^2=\sum_{n\ge0}\frac{m_0^2}{2^n(n+1)^2}\le2m_0^2 .
\end{equation}
For $m_0>0$ the first series diverges. The second is convergent power when $w_n$ is an amplitude. A finite corpus supplies one assignment per query at a given prefix. Summing occupancies over every prefix would count observations repeatedly. The zero-mass and negative-mass cases of the algebra should not be read as positive physical amounts.

\section{Design, nulls and results}
We used a base temperature of $\tau=0.05$ for ambient Gibbs scoring, with the reference-energy adjustment in Table~2. The focal-cube Hamming score and its Walsh reconstruction both used $\tau=0.75$. The globe Gibbs scores used $\tau=0.2$, and the semantic and PCA six-bit Hamming scores used $\tau=0.75$.

The fixture records two Workers AI passes, 18 batches of six texts per pass \citep{bge,cf}. One omitted pooling and one requested mean pooling. Both reported CLS in all batches. Ninety-six rows were componentwise identical; the minimum same-text cosine was 0.99999867. All stored top choices agreed. The provider responses support a repeatability test of the observed endpoint. They supply no comparison of two realized pooling modes.

Zeta-zero pair correlation and random-matrix spacing laws require their own hypotheses and ensembles \citep{montgomery}. We used the measured fixed-margin null.

\section{What is checked}
\code{OccupancyBounds.lean} contains 41 named theorems. It proves positivity and normalization, interchange of finite amounts, molar exponent conversion, replication, shell multiplicity and regrouping, finite and infinite dyadic claims, the sphere-lift norm identity, and the spherical cosine-energy identity given the right-triangle law. An octant witnesses the strict squared-arc inequality. It also proves sampled Gram expansion, finite Parseval under stated orthogonality, the heat amplitude-to-power factor, Walsh product-kernel factorization, finite convolution, orthogonal inversion and signed class-score reconstruction, as well as collision, log-ratio and gap bounds. The upstream compiler and mathlib versions are both 4.35.0 \citep{lean,mathlib}. In particular, \proved{cube_class_score} proves the direct-versus-spectral identity on every finite Boolean cube. \proved{cube_orthogonal} derives its orthogonality relation from the two one-bit signs, and \proved{cube_shifted_kernel} gives the transform for a kernel centered at any reference code. The hash-pinned scope manifest identifies every statement and the definitions on which it operates. The continuous spherical heat kernel, spherical cosine law and physical canonical model enter as cited mathematical background. The formal file does not derive a continuum physical model from embeddings.

\code{tools/occupancy/replay.py} recomputes both observed passes. It uses the repository point-map, Boltzmann collapse, sonometer, fixed-margin sampler, angular decomposer and Walsh--Krawtchouk routines. The empirical checks include agreement of direct and transformed scores, byte conservation, exact per-query classifications across passes and finite-precision inverse geometry. The computed statistics depend on text, model response, fixture order and sampling seeds. The dedicated CI workflow builds against the project's pinned upstream Lean and mathlib, checks axioms, and runs both datasets without a Cloudflare credential. The optional re-embedding command accepts a separately supplied credential for a fresh measurement.

What transfers from statistical mechanics is the accounting: specify the entity, preserve its amount and normalize a distribution over alternatives. Here that accounting exposes two distinct findings. The ambient chart resolved more directions at a meaningful fixed scale. On the heavily compressed cube, collisions survived a geometry null while signed degree alignment failed its class-label test. Native centroids provide a strong benchmark for the next test on independently collected notes.
