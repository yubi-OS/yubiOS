# Measurement sources and reuse

* **Handwriting.** E. Alpaydin and C. Kaynak, Optical Recognition of Handwritten Digits, UCI Machine Learning Repository, DOI [10.24432/C50P49](https://doi.org/10.24432/C50P49), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Selected public 8 × 8 count grids, serialized here as numerical text. The 30 original training writers and 13 test writers are distinct. A source ID is the original filename and its one-based data line. Source hashes cover the CSV line excluding its terminator.
* **Speech.** Jakobovski and contributors, [Free Spoken Digit Dataset](https://github.com/Jakobovski/free-spoken-digit-dataset/tree/26eb9aaf76e81b692f806f9140c2d2777410d7a1), [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Derived time-frequency grids and their associated study data are released under CC BY-SA 4.0 with attribution to the project and its speakers. Source hashes cover the original WAV files. The study uses a speaker-disjoint split different from the upstream recording-index convention.
* The mixed measurement collection and archived model outputs are distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. The recorded byte hashes identify observations. No raw voice recordings are redistributed. Only the numeric text field was submitted to Cloudflare.
